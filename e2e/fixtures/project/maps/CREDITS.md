# Art in this directory

The e2e fixture draws real tiles rather than generated colours, so the browser
suite proves that a sheet's rows and columns are cut correctly — arithmetic a
two-tile placeholder cannot exercise.

## Tiny 16: Basic

- **Files:** `tiny16-basic.png` (from `basictiles.png`), `tiny16-things.png` (from `things.png`)
- **Author:** Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm)
- **Source:** https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-16-basic
- **Licence:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  (also offered by the author as CC BY 3.0 and OGA-BY 3.0)
- **Attribution requested by the author:** "Please credit Lanea Zimmerman. The
  anti-DRM clause of CC-BY 3.0 is waived."
- **Changes:** none. The files are byte-for-byte as downloaded; only their names
  differ, so that two sheets from one pack do not collide with anything else in
  the fixture.

Both are 16×16 sheets: `tiny16-basic.png` is 128×240 (8 columns × 15 rows) and
`tiny16-things.png` is 192×128 (12 columns × 8 rows).

in `/LICENSE`. The two are separate works distributed together.
